# BE DIFFERENT iOS 1.2.0 (12) — Apple Guideline 2.3

Reason: Apple objected to App Store description promising coach communication and Different AI access while not available in the reviewed build. Submission: 4bc6e898-3acf-44ec-9f25-a464a45b6190. The fix is metadata only; no binary rebuild.

## en-US description

BE DIFFERENT brings training, recovery and activity tracking together.

Follow your performance score and data completeness. Plan your training, log exercises and track progress. Daily check ins, nutrition and recovery tools support informed everyday decisions.

Explore the exercise library, plan workouts and track your completed training sessions. Connect supported health data sources and wearables voluntarily. Missing data remains visible when a source is not connected.

Control your data through account settings, data export and account deletion. Health data is processed with your consent. The app provides training and lifestyle support and does not replace medical diagnosis or treatment.

## Review notes

We updated the English and German descriptions to accurately represent the features available to a standard athlete account in build 1.2.0 (12). In particular, the previous references to direct coach communication, Different AI assistance and personal coaching have been removed from the metadata. No in-app purchase is offered in this build. Please review the corrected metadata using the same build.
